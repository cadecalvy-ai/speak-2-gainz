# Speak 2 Gainz (S2G)

**Say what you lifted. Get the numbers to beat next time.**

S2G is a voice-first workout log. After the gym you tap one button and talk through the session the way you would tell a friend. It turns the ramble into exercises, sets, reps and weights, files it on a calendar, and tells you exactly what to beat on each lift next session.

> "Two sets of pull-ups to failure, got 13 then 12. Kelso shrugs, 25 a side, two sets of 11. Seated cable rows at 140 for 9 and 8..."

## Why

Most people stall for a boring reason: they don't remember what they did last time. "I think I did 75s, so I'll do 75s again." Logging every set on a phone between sets is the fix nobody keeps up. Talking for a minute afterwards, while cooking dinner, is one you can.

## What it does

- **One button.** Tap, talk, done. No forms, no exercise pickers.
- **Understands gym talk.** "25 a side", "the 30s", "to failure", supersets, rep ranges, and exercise names that speech-to-text garbles.
- **Asks instead of guessing.** Anything it had to guess, or that you never said, comes up as an approve / deny prompt before it is saved.
- **Tells you what to beat.** One more rep on one set. More weight only when every set tops the rep range and the jump is small enough to stay in it. Different advice when a lift dips or stalls.
- **Calendar and history.** Training days by type (push, pull, legs), and every lift's record over time.
- **Voice profile.** Set yourself up by saying who you are and what you are training for.

## Where it is going

- Growth charts: reps, total weight moved and strength over time
- Planning the next session from the program you already follow
- An iPhone app, and a widget that goes straight to recording
